Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers. No fluff. Everything you need to sign confidently.

How much does a Novexa site cost?
We offer tiered packages starting at $450. Your price depends on scope — number of products, integrations, and cinematic complexity. We recommend a short intake to give a fixed quote.
Do I need to provide content & images?
You provide logos and product images. We can generate or source mock imagery for previews. Final launch requires your assets or we’ll help create them at extra cost.
Will this work on phones?
Yes. All cinematic effects have mobile fallbacks and reduced particle load to keep performance high. We optimize after launch based on real feedback.
How long until launch?
Standard projects take 7–21 days depending on scope. Fast-turns are possible for extra fee.
Do you handle Shopify / product imports?
Yes — we scaffold stores, import CSVs or manually add SKUs depending on the package, and optimize checkout funnels for conversion.
What if I don’t like the design?
We include revision rounds defined per package. We work with you until the UX and feel match the brief.
